# Professional Statement & Portfolio Overview

## 1. Professional Statement

As a Master’s graduate in Computer Engineering with a strong passion for cybersecurity and GRC, I combine strong analytical problem-solving skills with practical expertise in network security and regulatory frameworks such as the NIS2 Directive and ISO 27001. Driven by values of integrity, proactive defense, and empathy in security, I bridge the gap between technical infrastructure requirements and the people they protect. My goal is to leverage my skills in vulnerability assessment, risk management, and compliance to help organizations safeguard sensitive data, fortify their defenses, and achieve long-term cyber resilience.
