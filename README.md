# Cybersecurity Portfolio

This repository documents my learning and practical work throughout the **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate**. It brings together course notes, exercises, and reports that apply core cybersecurity concepts to risk management, network security, access control, incident analysis, and automation.

The portfolio reflects how I have developed my understanding of cybersecurity and how I approach security challenges: by combining technical analysis with risk-based reasoning and clear communication. The exercises are educational and do not represent assessments of real-world systems.

## About me

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Engineering and am particularly interested in cybersecurity, network security, and **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)**. Through this portfolio, I explore how to identify and protect assets, assess risks and vulnerabilities, investigate incidents, and document practical security recommendations.

For more about my professional interests, values, and goals, see my [professional statement](./01-foundations-of-cybersecurity/professional-statement.md).

## Areas of focus

- **Risk management and GRC:** identifying assets, assessing threats and vulnerabilities, prioritizing risks, and recommending controls.
- **Network security:** TCP/IP protocols, traffic analysis, hardening, monitoring, and investigation of network incidents.
- **Linux and SQL:** managing permissions and using queries to filter and analyze data.
- **Detection and response:** triaging alerts, investigating evidence, documenting findings, and escalating incidents.
- **Python for security:** automating repetitive tasks and processing lists and files.
- **Professional communication:** presenting findings, potential impact, and recommendations in a clear and structured way.

## How to explore this repository

1. Open the folder for the course you would like to explore.
2. Start with that folder's `README.md` for an overview and links to its materials.
3. Review the exercises and reports highlighted above; the course notes provide additional context.
